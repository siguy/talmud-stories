# Apply Jeff's 2026-10-07 rulings

**Self-contained.** Read [`FRAMEWORK.md`](../../FRAMEWORK.md) first, then this.

## The claim to test / the problem

Jeff answered seven of the eight passages in the 2026-10-02 email
(`jeff comms/10-7-2026/Brief response, 10.7.docx`) and told Simon his recent rulings
outrank his 2005 lists. Three of his answers reject entries on those lists. The rules,
the goldens and the recall denominators must all follow his answers.

## Method

Rules into `docs/STORY_RULES.md` in his words; goldens by an idempotent apply script;
list entries annotated (never edited) in `results/expert_lists/superseded_by_rule.json`,
read by the recall harness, the board and the consensus judge; 4B re-asked.

## How you know it worked

`tests/test_superseded_by_rule.py` and the golden counts in `tests/test_bookkeeping.py`
pass; the apply script is a no-op on a second run.

## Guardrails

Never edit a 2005 list. A `borderline` stays in the corpus and the denominator.
