# Detector v14 against v11 on full Yevamot

**Self-contained.** A fresh session executes this with no other context.
Read [`FRAMEWORK.md`](../../FRAMEWORK.md) first, then this.

## The claim to test / the problem

The 2026-11-07 slice ([finding](../../docs/findings/2026-11-07-detector-v13-slice.md)) measured
v14 on 20 Yevamot pages only. Does the slice picture (equal recall once 78b:9 is set aside;
an R-S1 and an R-B4 error) hold on all 242 pages?

## Method

Scratch, uncommitted copy of `scripts/run_new_tractate.py` with its two `story_detector_v11`
imports pointed at v14 (the committed runner stays on v11, pinned by a test). Defaults:
`gemini-3-flash-preview`, thinking off, twin pass on, `TWIN_TRIGGER=all`; Stage 1 from the
cached `results/triage/yevamot.json`. Two full runs. Score them and the three existing v11
full runs with `measure_recall_vs_expert_list.py --expert-filter recall --matcher exact`;
compare proposals with `scripts/compare_v14_v11_yevamot.py`.

## How you know it worked

Stage 1 cache byte-identical before and after; 0 Stage 2 errors and 0 twin failures in
`run_meta`; five recall files from one harness.

## Guardrails

Do not switch the runner. Cost stop at $12 per run.

## When done

Write the finding to `docs/findings/<date>-<slug>.md`, add an `## Outcome` section
below, and `git mv` this file to `work/done/`. **Never delete it.**

## Outcome

Done 2026-11-08. Recall is a tie inside the noise: v11 93, 93, 94 / v14 95, 93 of 100,
with no list story found by every run of one arm and none of the other. The proposals move
in both directions: v14 drops bare reports, habits and cases brought to a rabbi (the
October lines doing their job), but takes in ordinary scholarly dialogue as borderline and
keeps biblical material (David 2 of 2; a new Midian/Jabesh-Gilead passage). First
written as "the runner stays on v11"; **corrected the same day (Simon): the runner moves to
v14.** Why: what v14 removes is Jeff's October rulings, what it adds is mostly borderline
(kept for review), and v11 makes the David error too. The biblical error is to be fixed
post hoc in the Stage 4j filter (a v15). The comparison is confounded — the v11 runs date from
September and predate PRs #69/#71 — so what moved is **indicated**, not attributed to the
wording. Finding: [`2026-11-08-v14-vs-v11-yevamot`](../../docs/findings/2026-11-08-v14-vs-v11-yevamot.md).
