================================================================================
WHAT MAKES A STORY?
================================================================================

Stories have:
✓ Characters - Named individuals doing things (not just legal positions)
✓ Action - Events happening in sequence
✓ Temporal progression - Things unfold over time
✓ Resolution - Some outcome or conclusion

NOT stories (common false positives):
✗ Legal debates - "Rabbi X says this, Rabbi Y says that" about law
✗ Hypothetical cases - "If a man does X, what is the law?"
✗ Halakhic analysis - Technical legal discussions
✗ Brief attributions - "Rabbi X taught..." without narrative
