**2. How we are now measuring ourselves.** We split the work into four capabilities that
can fail separately, so a problem gets fixed where it lives instead of blamed on "the AI":

- **Triage** — is this page worth reading at all? (It discards half the tractate; a page
  never read leaves no trace, so this one is measured first.)
- **Detection** — propose every passage that might be a story. It proposes; it does not judge.
- **Classification** — is it really a story, by your criteria?
- **Boundaries** — where exactly does it start and end?

They compose: what we lose in Triage cannot be recovered later. On Gittin, Triage lost
none of your 112.
