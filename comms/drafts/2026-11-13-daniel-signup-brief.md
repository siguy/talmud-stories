# Learning the stories of the Talmud in memory of Joe Septimus — how sign-up will work

*Draft for Daniel. Please edit or comment anywhere.*

## The idea

Family, friends and community commit to a small amount of learning each week, from launch until the yahrzeit (8 Elul), and together learn every story in the Talmud.

## The size of it

- About 1,800 stories across 37 tractates. All of Shas has been run through the story finder. **This number is not final:** the list is still being checked with Jeff Rubenstein (it stood at 1,784 on 13 November) and will move up or down a little as stories are added or removed. The list people learn from will be dated, and we can update it without anyone losing their place.
- Roughly 400,000 words of English translation, plus the Hebrew/Aramaic.
- At a slow study pace (reading Hebrew and English, pausing to think; we are assuming about 75 English words a minute), that is roughly 90 hours of learning in total.
- Spread over about 40 weeks, the whole group needs a little over 2 hours a week combined.
- So around 13-14 people doing 10 minutes a week complete all of Shas once. Ten minutes a week is about 3 stories.

What this means: we will almost certainly have many more people than one pass needs. That is good news, and it is the main thing we need your view on (question 1).

## How it works for a participant

1. Open the site, read about Joe and the project, sign in with Google. No other questions.
2. Choose how much time to give each week (for example 10, 20 or 40 minutes).
3. Everyone starts the same week, on a fixed launch date. Sign-ups open before that.
4. Each week they get an email with that week's stories and links. The same list is on their own "my stories" page when signed in.
5. They read each story (Hebrew with vowels and English side by side) and tap "done".
6. Stories are given out in order through Shas, so each person's stories sit next to each other.
7. If someone misses a week, their stories stay on their list for about two more weeks, then go back to be given to someone else. Nothing is lost.
8. Everyone can see overall progress.

## Not in the first version (on purpose, to launch simply)

- A "give me a story" button for people who want to learn without committing. We plan to add it after launch, to cover anything left open and for people who want to do more.
- Ways for people who read the same story to get more context and talk to each other.
- An "ask a question about this story" AI helper.

## Questions for you

1. **What does "finishing" mean with many readers?** Since a small group covers Shas once, should the goal be (a) the community completing Shas many times together, with a counter ("completed 7 times"), or (b) each person on their own path through the stories? We lean towards (a), starting each new person where the last one's stories end, so the first full pass is done quickly.
2. **Launch date:** when should learning start, and how long should sign-ups be open first?
3. **The yahrzeit:** is 8 Elul right, and which date should be the finish?
4. **The weekly email:** who should it come from, and what tone?
5. **Reading pace:** does about 75 words a minute (10 minutes ≈ 3 stories) feel right, or should a week's portion be lighter or heavier?
6. **The page about your father:** would you write it, or send us what you would like it to say?
7. **Communities:** which should get their own sign-up link (Hadar, DN, Kesher, Brooklyn, family...) so we can show who took part?
