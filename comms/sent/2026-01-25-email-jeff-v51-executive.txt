KEY CHANGES IN v5.1
================================================================================

1. NEW DISQUALIFIER: Rabbi Legal Opinion (Your #1 Pattern)

   Your insight: "Whenever it sees a rabbi's name saying something, it assumes
   this rabbi is a character in a story. But in most cases the rabbi is just
   discussing a legal case."

   Solution: New automatic disqualifier catches:
   - "Rabbi X said that it is permitted..."
   - "Rabbi X quotes Rabbi Y as saying..."
   - "Rabbi X discusses a case of..."

   Impact: Applied 53 times on pages 2-39 (your major improvement)

2. STRICTER CAUSALITY TEST (Your Sequential Events Pattern)

   Your example: Girl drew water, was raped = sequential, NOT causal

   OLD: "Events connected by cause and effect" (vague)
   NEW: "Event A CAUSED Event B, which CAUSED Event C" (explicit)

   Result: Your Ketubot 14b example now correctly rejected

3. STRICTER CHANGE TEST (Your Simple Report Pattern)

   Your examples: Levi wedding visit, Rav Ashi recited blessings

   OLD: "Situation different at end than beginning" (vague)
   NEW: "Situation TRANSFORMED from beginning to end" (explicit)

   Result: Simple action reports without transformation now rejected

4. BOUNDARY DETECTION (Your Too Long/Too Short Patterns)

   Commentary markers (fixes "too long"):
   - טַעְמָא דְּ (ta'ama de) - "the reason is..."
   - "The Gemara explains..." (story ends before meta-discussion)

   Continuation markers (fixes "too short"):
   - זִמְנָא אַחֲרִינָא - "On another occasion"
   - Extends story across segments when appropriate

5. CATEGORICAL CLASSIFICATION

   Replaced percentage scores with actionable categories:
   - YES: All 6 criteria, no weakeners (definitive)
   - HIGH_CONFIDENCE: 5-6 criteria, some weakeners
   - LOW_CONFIDENCE: 3-4 criteria (needs review)
   - NOT_A_STORY: <3 criteria OR any disqualifier

   Shows exactly which criteria met/failed with evidence.
