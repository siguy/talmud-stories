## Jeff's v4.1 False Positive Patterns

### 1. Simple Reports (No Causality/Change)

**Problem:** AI accepted ceremonial/procedural reports as stories even without transformation.

**Jeff's Examples:**
- ❌ #1 (Ketubot 2b): "Levi visited Rabbi's house during wedding" → NO change
- ❌ #2 (Ketubot 8a): "Rav Ashi attended wedding and recited blessings" → NO change
- ❌ #4 (Ketubot 8a): "Rav Ḥaviva came to circumcision and recited blessing" → NO causality or change

**v5.1 Solution:**
- New weakener: `simple_report` for "X came and did Y" without causality/change
- Stricter CHANGE test: Must show transformation, not just action report
- Stricter CAUSALITY test: Must trace causal chain, not sequential events

**v5.1 Results on Jeff's Pages:**

*Ketubot 2b:*
- Found: 1 HIGH_CONFIDENCE story (ferry divorce case - 6/6 criteria)
- Different story than Jeff's "Levi wedding visit" false positive
- Likely rejected similar simple reports

*Ketubot 8a:*
- Found: 1 LOW_CONFIDENCE story (Ravina marriage arrangement)
- Summary: "Ravina arranged a marriage for his son and recited the blessing from betrothal, but the marriage ultimately failed"
- **Key difference:** This has actual change (marriage failed), unlike Jeff's false positive examples
- Correctly classified as LOW (not HIGH) due to borderline nature

**Assessment:** ✅ v5.1 correctly applying stricter criteria

### 2. Rabbi Legal Opinions (Attribution vs Character)

**Problem:** AI confused rabbis ATTRIBUTING legal opinions with rabbis as CHARACTERS in stories.

**Jeff's Critical Insight:**
> "Whenever it sees a rabbi's name saying something, it assumes this rabbi is a character in a story. But in most cases the rabbi is just discussing a legal case and his name is just to attribute the legal ruling to him."

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Rabbi Shmuel quotes Rabbi Yonatan as saying it is permitted..." → ATTRIBUTION
- ❌ "Rabbi Avin discusses a case of shooting an arrow..." → LEGAL DISCUSSION
- ✅ "A man came before Rava and Rava ruled..." → CHARACTER

**v5.1 Solution:**
New disqualifier: `rabbi_legal_opinion`

Detection markers:
- "said that it is permitted/prohibited"
- "quotes Rabbi X as saying"
- "discusses a case of"
- "The Gemara questions"

**v5.1 Results:**
- **Applied 8 times** across Ketubot 2a-10b
- Successfully caught attribution cases and rejected as NOT_A_STORY

**Example from v5.1 output (Ketubot 2a, segments 1-4):**
```
Classification: NOT_A_STORY
Disqualifiers: rabbi_legal_opinion
Reasoning: "This is a discussion of legal reasoning and the basis for a
legal ruling. The rabbis are stating legal opinions and analyzing the
mishna, not characters in a narrative."
```

**Assessment:** ✅ Major improvement - entire class of false positives now being caught

### 3. Sequential Events (Without Causation)

**Problem:** AI accepted events happening in sequence as stories, even without causal connection.

**Jeff's Example (Ketubot 14b #7):**
> "Girl drew water. Girl was raped."
>
> "There are two events but **not a causal relationship** or change. The narrative relates that two events happened, but there is **no causal relationship that is necessary for a story**."

**v5.1 Solution:**

**Old Standard (v4):**
- "Events connected by cause and effect" (vague)

**New Standard (v5.1):**
```
CAUSAL_CHAIN (STRICT):
- REQUIRED: Event A CAUSED Event B, which CAUSED Event C
- ✗ INSUFFICIENT: "Two events happened" (sequential without causation)
- Example FAIL: "Girl drew water. Girl was raped." (2 events, NO causal link)
- Example PASS: "Didn't return → wife distressed → tear → death" (each causes next)
```

**Self-Check Question Enhanced:**
```
Old: "Can I state the causal chain?"
New: "Are events CAUSAL or just SEQUENTIAL?"
     - "X happened, then Y happened" (sequential) ≠ story
     - "X caused Y, which caused Z" (causal) = story
```

**v5.1 Results:**
- New weakener `minimal_causality` for borderline cases
- Note: Ketubot 14b not in test range (pages 2-10), so can't verify this specific example
- Stricter causality test being applied in all classifications

**Assessment:** ✅ Framework in place - needs testing on Ketubot 14b to verify

### 4. Boundary Issues

**Problem:** AI included Talmud's commentary ABOUT the story as part of the story (5 cases "too_long"), or split multi-segment stories (2 cases "too_short").

**Jeff's Examples:**
- #3: Should end with "וְהָדְרִי בְּהוּ" (and they retracted), NOT include later analysis
- #18: Should omit "טַעְמָא דְּלָא אֲתוֹ עֵדִים" (Talmud's reasoning)
- #21 & #22: Same story continues "On another occasion..."

**v5.1 Solution:**

**Story End Markers (stop before meta-discussion):**
```python
TALMUD_COMMENTARY_MARKERS = {
    "hebrew": [
        "וְלֵית הִלְכְתָא",  # and the law is not...
        "טַעְמָא דְּ",  # the reason is...
        "מַאי טַעְמָא",  # what is the reason...
    ],
    "english": [
        "And the halakha is not in accordance",
        "The reason that",
        "The Gemara explains",
    ]
}
```

**Continuation Markers (extend across segments):**
```python
CONTINUATION_MARKERS = {
    "hebrew": ["זִמְנָא אַחֲרִינָא", "פַּעַם אַחֶרֶת"],
    "english": ["On another occasion", "Another time"]
}
```

**v5.1 Results:**
- Boundary detection implemented in code
- No obvious boundary issues in Ketubot 2-10 results
- Would need Jeff's validation of specific pages to verify improvement

**Assessment:** ⏳ Implementation complete - needs validation
