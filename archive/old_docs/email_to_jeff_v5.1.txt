Subject: v5.1 Story Detection: Your v4.1 Validation Improvements Implemented

Hi Jeff,

I've implemented v5.1 of the Talmud story detection system based entirely on the patterns you identified in your v4.1 validation (the 30 stories with 15 TRUE, 15 FALSE positives).

Your feedback was incredibly valuable - particularly your insight about the rabbi attribution vs character confusion: "Whenever it sees a rabbi's name saying something, it assumes this rabbi is a character in a story. But in most cases the rabbi is just discussing a legal case and his name is just to attribute the legal ruling to him."

WHAT'S NEW IN v5.1

I've addressed all the major false positive patterns you identified:

1. New Disqualifier: Rabbi Legal Opinion
   - Catches rabbis ATTRIBUTING legal opinions vs rabbis as CHARACTERS
   - Detection: "said that it is permitted", "quotes", "discusses a case of"
   - Already working: Applied 8 times in Ketubot 2-10 test

2. Stricter Causality Test
   Your example of the girl drawing water/being raped was perfect:
   - Old (vague): "Events connected by cause and effect"
   - New (explicit): "Event A CAUSED Event B, which CAUSED Event C"
   - Self-check now asks: "Are events CAUSAL or just SEQUENTIAL?"

3. Stricter Change Test
   Your simple report examples (Levi wedding visit, Rav Ashi recited blessings):
   - Old (vague): "Situation different at end than beginning"
   - New (explicit): "Situation TRANSFORMED from beginning to end"
   - Self-check now asks: "Is this a TRANSFORMATION or just a REPORT?"

4. New Weakeners for Granularity
   - simple_report, minimal_causality, minimal_change
   - Allow borderline cases → LOW_CONFIDENCE instead of being rejected

5. Boundary Detection
   - Commentary markers: Stop before "the reason is...", "The Gemara explains"
   - Continuation markers: Extend across "on another occasion"

6. Enhanced Self-Check (6 → 7 tests)
   - New: Rabbi character test (attribution vs character)
   - Enhanced: Causality test (causal vs sequential)
   - Enhanced: Change test (transformation vs report)
