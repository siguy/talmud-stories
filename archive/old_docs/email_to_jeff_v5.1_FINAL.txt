WHAT'S NEW IN v5.1

Your feedback drove 7 major improvements:

1. New Disqualifier: Rabbi Legal Opinion (53 applications!)
   Your insight: "Whenever it sees a rabbi's name saying something, it assumes
   this rabbi is a character in a story. But in most cases the rabbi is just
   discussing a legal case."

   Detection: "said that it is permitted", "quotes", "discusses a case of"

2. Stricter Causality Test
   Your example: Girl drew water, was raped = sequential, NOT causal

   Old: "Events connected by cause and effect" (vague)
   New: "Event A CAUSED Event B, which CAUSED Event C" (explicit)
   Result: Ketubot 14b correctly rejected as NOT_A_STORY

3. Stricter Change Test
   Your examples: Levi wedding visit, Rav Ashi recited blessings = reports, NOT transformations

   Old: "Situation different at end than beginning" (vague)
   New: "Situation TRANSFORMED from beginning to end" (explicit)

4. New Weakeners
   - minimal_change: 26 applications
   - minimal_causality: 23 applications
   - simple_report: 6 applications

   Impact: Better granularity (borderline → LOW instead of HIGH)

5. Boundary Detection
   - Commentary markers: "the reason is...", "The Gemara explains"
   - Continuation markers: "on another occasion"

6. Enhanced Self-Check (6 → 7 tests)
   - New: Rabbi character test
   - Enhanced: Causality (causal vs sequential)
   - Enhanced: Change (transformation vs report)
