Berakhot — 10 random stories (unreviewed tractate) — Story Review

Random sample of the 137 shipped stories. Check 1: is it a story? Check 2: open 'more' and mark if the start or end is wrong — ends are the known weak side.

Hebrew and English are shown side by side, one row per segment, so the two always cover the same extent. Nothing is cut in either language — the story is highlighted inside the full text.

If an entry is right, click Yes and move on — that is the whole review for it.
If something is off, use “Something else is wrong” to say what: the extent, the confidence level, or whether it should be split or merged. A passage can be a story and have the wrong extent — those are separate questions on purpose, because we have been unable to tell them apart and it has sent us fixing the wrong things.
If the extent is wrong, a box opens for the Hebrew: highlight the words in the text above and press “Use highlighted text” — no need to type them. There are two boxes, where the story should start and where it should end; fill either or both. Below them you can instead point at one passage and say whether it belongs in the story or should be cut.
Highlighted rows are the segments the story sits in. Where part of the Hebrew is also marked like this, the mark is our claimed extent — the exact words we say the story starts and ends with — and the rest of the highlight is context. Judge the boundary against the mark.
Keyboard (when not typing in a box): j jumps to the next unreviewed entry, then y / b / n answer Yes / Borderline / No for it.
If the page itself is broken — text missing, Hebrew not matching — click ⚠ Display problem. That is our bug, not a judgement about the passage.
