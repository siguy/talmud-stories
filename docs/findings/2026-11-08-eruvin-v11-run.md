## Follow-up: why triage lost 14b and 32b, and what the 53b pair is

**Triage (measured from the cached labels, no new API calls).** Stage 1 labelled *no* segment on
either page NARRATIVE_EVENT. 14b is 13 DELIBERATION + 7 VERBAL_ACT; 32b is 8 DELIBERATION + 2
VERBAL_ACT. The shipped rule (R-T1) keeps a page only on >=1 NARRATIVE_EVENT; verbal acts alone never
keep it. The scene-setter of 32b ("R. Hiyya bar Abba sat, and with him sat R. Asi...") was read as
DELIBERATION. So the loss is a labelling call, not a rule bug.

Triage skipped 101 of 207 pages. Pages skipped with at least k verbal acts: k=2 -> 72 pages, k=4 -> 42
pages. Keeping the k=4 pages would have recovered 14b only; k=2 would have recovered both, at ~72 extra
Stage 2 calls. Neither is a principled boundary (Lesson 37: V>=4 was already rejected as a fitted
threshold). Eruvin's triage loss, 2/74 = 2.7%, is in line with Ketubot (2/149) and Kiddushin (2/90).
*Indicated: two pages.* (Ref note: the list's `Eruvin 67a` entry is located on 67b; nothing is lost
there. Only 14b and 32b are true triage losses.)

**53b (R. Yose bar Asyan, R. Abbahu).** Jeff lists three consecutive entries of one shape: the
maidservant of Rabbi's house (eruvin_040), Yose bar Asyan (041), Abbahu (042), each "when X spoke in
the language of the sages, he would say [a riddle]". The page was examined. The detector proposed the
maidservant (segment 8, YES) and proposed *nothing* on the other two (segments 10 and 12). So the same
shape is treated two ways inside one run: a consistency gap, not a scope disagreement. Both runs agree.
R-C3/R-C5 may explain it (a habit that leads nowhere stays a custom), but Abbahu's entry ends with the
rabbis' retort and his reply. *Suspected*, not diagnosed.
