# Detector v14 against v11 on Berakhot, ruled on by the consensus judges — 2026-11-08

**On the passages that moved, both judges side with v14: 14 of 20, against 1 for v11, and
5 split.** v14 keeps fewer proposals than v11 (189 and 187 against 201). What it drops is
mostly what the October rulings were written to drop: habits, bare reports, and
question-and-answer with nothing else happening. Over all 224 passages either version
proposed, both judges agree with v14 a little more often than with v11 (Gemini 84.8% vs
83.9%, Claude 73.2% vs 67.9%, kept-vs-not). Both versions make the same R-S1 error,
kept by **both** in every run: five biblical episodes that both judges call out of scope.
Berakhot has **no expert list**, so this has **no recall** and **no ground truth**: the judges
are two models reading the register, not Jeff. Everything about who is right is **indicated**.

## The prompt lags the register

`judge_register_v2.md` says borderline includes *"one event followed by discussion or a
ruling."* Jeff withdrew that shape on 2026-10-25, and the register the prompt carries now
says so (R-C7 shape 2, R-C0's limit). No prompt version has been written since. So the
judge gets two instructions that disagree. It shows: Gemini keeps 38b:9 as `story`, citing
*"an incident followed by a ruling counts as a story."* That leans Gemini toward v11 on the
split passages. It does not touch the 14 where both judges agree. **suspected**, not
measured: no arm runs a corrected prompt. A `judge_register_v4.md` that drops the
withdrawn shape is the fix; it would change every earlier run's prompt SHA, so it gets a
new file, not an edit.

## What this says

1. **On Berakhot, the adoption holds.** v14 keeps 12–14 fewer passages. On the passages that
   moved, both judges agree with its call 14 times to 1, and by the register most of the
   drops are R-C5/R-C3/R-C2 cases Jeff ruled out. (**indicated**: no ground truth; the
   judges are not Jeff)
2. **v14 is not stricter everywhere.** Two of its three additions are bare reports that the
   judges and the register reject (24a:22, 39b:9). It also drops one real story, the
   re-quoted R. Tarfon incident (11a:22), as a "citation". (**indicated**)
3. **What neither version fixes:** five biblical episodes in every run (R-S1), and 11
   habits, reports and exchanges kept by both. The first is the planned v15 post-hoc filter; this finding
   gives it five Berakhot cases to catch, all with group or *God* actors. (**measured** that
   they are kept; **indicated** that they are wrong)
4. **v14's own noise is small.** The two runs differ by 2 kept stories and by 0–2 points of
   judge agreement. With one v11 run, the v11 side's noise is unmeasured (Lesson 22).
