## What it is

After Stage 2, for each story found, look at the free segment on either side and ask one
narrow question: *here is the story we found; here is the segment beside it — a separate
incident, the same story, or not a story?* Add on `separate_incident` only. Its own
method, its own prompt; the page-level detection prompt is byte-identical with the pass
on or off (`tests/test_twin_pass.py` pins it, after the 2026-09-09 lesson).

Two trigger settings. **`labelled`** asks only where Stage 1 marked the neighbour
narrative or verbal act. **`all`** asks about every free neighbour.

## Why this worked where the broad instruction did not

"Find every story on this page" saturates: the model returns a representative of a run
and stops, and telling it harder does not change that. "Is *this* passage a separate
incident from *that* one" is a comparison with both texts in front of it. The mechanism
matches the defect — 18 of 38 misses were one segment from a proposal — rather than
restating the goal.
