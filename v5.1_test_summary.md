## v5.1 Improvements Working

### 1. Rabbi Legal Opinion Disqualifier ✅

**Problem (Jeff's insight):**
> "Whenever it sees a rabbi's name saying something, it assumes this rabbi is a character in a story. But in most cases the rabbi is just discussing a legal case."

**v5.1 Solution:**
New disqualifier catches:
- "Rabbi X said that it is permitted..."
- "Rabbi X quotes Rabbi Y as saying..."
- "Rabbi X discusses a case of..."

**Results:**
Applied 8 times in Ketubot 2-10, correctly rejecting legal attribution as NOT_A_STORY.

**Examples:**
- Ketubot 2a: "Rav Yosef, Rav Yehuda, and Shmuel discuss the reason for the mishna's ruling" → NOT_A_STORY ✓
- Ketubot 4a: "Rabbis state opinions about meat sales when death occurs" → NOT_A_STORY ✓
- Ketubot 9b: "Rav Yehuda says Shmuel says a groom claiming 'unobstructed orifice' is believed" → NOT_A_STORY ✓

---

### 2. Stricter Causality Test ✅

**Problem (Jeff's example):**
> "Girl drew water. Girl was raped. Two events but NOT a causal relationship."

**v5.1 Solution:**
```
Old: "Events connected by cause and effect" (vague)
New: "Event A CAUSED Event B, which CAUSED Event C" (explicit)
```

Self-check now asks:
- "Are events CAUSAL or just SEQUENTIAL?"
- "X then Y" (sequential) ≠ story
- "X caused Y caused Z" (causal) = story

**Status:** Framework implemented. Needs testing on Ketubot 14b (Jeff's specific example).

---

### 3. Stricter Change Test ✅

**Problem (Jeff's examples):**
- "Levi visited Rabbi's house during wedding" → NO change
- "Rav Ashi attended wedding and recited blessings" → NO change

**v5.1 Solution:**
```
Old: "Situation different at end than beginning" (vague)
New: "Situation TRANSFORMED from beginning to end" (explicit)
```

Self-check now asks:
- "Is this a TRANSFORMATION or just a REPORT?"
- Simple report ("came and recited blessing") = NO change → NOT_A_STORY
- Actual transformation ("friends close → friends distanced") = Change → Story

**New weakener:** `minimal_change` for borderline cases (applied to Ketubot 8b mourner story)

---

### 4. New Weakeners for Granularity ✅

Three new weakeners allow borderline cases to be LOW_CONFIDENCE instead of incorrectly HIGH:

1. **simple_report**: "X came and did Y" without causality/change
2. **minimal_causality**: Causality present but barely meets threshold
3. **minimal_change**: Change present but barely transformative

**Impact:** Better confidence granularity, more human review of borderline cases

---

### 5. Boundary Detection

**Problem:**
- 5 cases "too_long" (included Talmud's commentary)
- 2 cases "too_short" (split multi-segment stories)

**v5.1 Solution:**

Commentary markers (story ended, stop before analysis):
- Hebrew: "טַעְמָא דְּ" (the reason is...)
- English: "The Gemara explains..."

Continuation markers (same story across segments):
- Hebrew: "זִמְנָא אַחֲרִינָא" (another occasion)
- English: "On another occasion"

**Status:** Implemented. Needs validation on Jeff's specific boundary issue pages.
